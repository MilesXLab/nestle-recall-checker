## Executive Summary

This project is **open source for community use** but **protected against commercial exploitation**. We welcome non-commercial contributions and deployments, but any commercial use requires explicit written permission and a commercial license agreement.

---

## 1. What Is Permitted (Free, Non-Commercial Use)

✅ **Personal & Family Use**
- Individuals can use this tool to check product safety for themselves and family
- No restrictions on sharing with friends or community members

✅ **Educational & Research**
- Students, educators, and researchers can use this for learning
- Publishing academic research based on the data is allowed (with attribution)

✅ **Community & Non-Profit Deployment**
- Non-profit organizations can deploy this tool for their constituents
- Community groups can host and maintain instances
- Public health agencies can use and modify for public safety

✅ **Open Source Contributions**
- Developers can fork, modify, and submit improvements
- Community can build derivative educational projects
- Contributing improvements back is encouraged

✅ **Internal Institutional Use**
- Hospitals, clinics, and health centers can deploy internally
- Government agencies can use for public safety
- Universities can use for research and education

---

## 2. What Is NOT Permitted (Requires Commercial License)

❌ **Selling Access**
- Charging users for access to the tool
- Premium versions, paywalls, or subscription fees
- Monetizing through feature locks or content restrictions

❌ **Commercial Services**
- Offering as part of a commercial SaaS platform
- Including in commercial product bundles
- Providing as a commercial API or consulting service

❌ **Rebranding for Profit**
- Removing TechDadShanghai attribution
- Claiming authorship or original creation
- Republishing with different branding to commercialize

❌ **Indirect Monetization**
- Using as lead generation for commercial ventures
- Including in a paid app (even if other features are paid)
- Revenue sharing or affiliate arrangements without permission

## 3. Commercial Licensing Process

If you want to use this project commercially, here's the process:

### Step 1: Contact Us
📧 **Email:** opensource@techdadshanghai.com

Include:
- Organization name and description
- Intended commercial use case
- Target market and scale
- Business model (SaaS, embedded, API, etc.)

### Step 2: License Negotiation
We typically offer flexible commercial licenses:
- **Start-up License:** Lower fees for early-stage companies
- **Enterprise License:** Volume and support-based pricing
- **OEM/Integration License:** For embedding in other products
- **Exclusive License:** Regional or market-specific exclusivity (negotiable)

### Step 3: Legal Agreement
A formal commercial license agreement will be prepared covering:
- Permitted uses and restrictions
- Attribution requirements
- Warranty and support terms
- Royalty or fixed fee structure
- Term and termination conditions

### Step 4: Implementation
Once agreed:
- License key or documentation provided
- Support available for integration
- Updates and improvements included (depending on agreement)

## 4. Key Terms for Commercial Licensees

If you obtain a commercial license, you must:

✓ **Maintain Attribution**
- Keep visible credit to TechDadShanghai
- Include in documentation, UI, or promotional materials
- Cannot remove or obscure original authorship

✓ **Follow License Terms**
- Use only for agreed-upon commercial purposes
- Respect territorial or market limitations if specified
- Report usage metrics or revenue as agreed

✓ **Provide Support**
- Ensure tool availability and performance
- Implement proper error handling and security
- Report critical bugs to the original author

✓ **Protect Public Safety**
- Maintain data accuracy and verification processes
- Do not distribute false or misleading health information
- Implement proper disclaimers and safety warnings

## 5. Specific Commercial Scenarios

### Scenario 1: B2C SaaS Platform
**Example:** Launching a parenting app with formula safety checker

**Status:** ❌ NOT PERMITTED without license
**Commercial License Required:** Yes
**Typical Approach:** 
- Negotiate revenue share or fixed license fee
- Agreement includes product integration rights

### Scenario 2: Health Care Provider Deployment
**Example:** Hospital using tool for patient education

**Status:** ✅ PERMITTED (can be free/internal)
**Commercial License Required:** No (unless selling to others)
**Terms:** 
- Internal use is free
- If you offer to other hospitals for a fee → Commercial license needed

### Scenario 3: Government/Public Health Agency
**Example:** National health ministry deploying to citizens

**Status:** ✅ PERMITTED
**Commercial License Required:** No
**Terms:**
- Public deployment is always free for public health
- Modification and localization welcome
- Attribution appreciated but not legally required

### Scenario 4: Educational Content Creator
**Example:** YouTuber creating video about formula safety

**Status:** ✅ PERMITTED (even with monetized channel)
**Commercial License Required:** No
**Terms:**
- Educational use is permitted
- Credit/link to project appreciated
- Cannot claim exclusive ownership

### Scenario 5: Corporate Rebranding
**Example:** Large consumer company wants to rebrand as their own tool

**Status:** ❌ NOT PERMITTED
**Commercial License Required:** Yes (if they want to proceed)
**Typical Approach:**
- Premium commercial license required
- Likely higher cost due to commercial sensitivity
- Negotiation based on market and revenue

## 6. Enforcement & Dispute Resolution

### Monitoring
- We actively monitor for unauthorized commercial use

- Community can report violations

### Violations
If commercial use is detected without license:
1. **First Notice:** Cease-and-desist letter and opportunity to license
2. **Second Stage:** Formal legal action to remove content
3. **Final:** Copyright infringement claims and damages

### Fair Dealing
We understand:
- Some gray areas exist (e.g., is an internal corporate use "commercial"?)
- Good faith implementation is appreciated
- When in doubt, asking permission is appreciated
